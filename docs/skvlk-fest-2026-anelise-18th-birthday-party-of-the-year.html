Inside SKVLK Fest 2026: Anelise's 18th Birthday Bash Was the Party of the Year

Carnival rides, a stacked lineup and a VIP creator lounge: Jamie Foxx and his daughter Anelise threw Thousand Oaks a night to remember. And the stabbing story? The festival's team says it never happened. Were you there?

By Hollywood News Access · November 10, 2026

Some kids get a cake for their 18th. Anelise got a festival. SKVLK Fest, the Halloween music festival she created, took over the family's Thousand Oaks home on Saturday night, and with her dad Jamie Foxx behind her, it was the party everybody wanted to be at.

Think carnival rides, a glowing main stage, red carpet entrances and a VIP creator lounge styled by Dolls Kill, where creators filmed their looks and went live all night. Entertainment Weekly reported a lineup including Offset, OhGeesy, 310babii and SuperDuperKyle. Organizers say more than 25,000 people came through.

"It was amazing," said Terry Matari Bryant of PR STARPOWER, the festival's publicist, who was there all night. "The community loved it, the young people loved it, and Jamie and Anelise put on a fantastic show."

Jamie Foxx clearly agreed: he posted videos from the night praising his daughter.

About that stabbing story

By Sunday, TMZ was reporting, citing law enforcement sources, that a guest had been stabbed, and the story spread fast. The festival's team says it is false. "No one was stabbed," PR STARPOWER said in an official statement. Two teenagers had a brief scuffle, one got a minor scratch, and Ventura County deputies responded, according to the statement. A Sheriff's spokesperson told the Daily Mail a juvenile was arrested. The team has asked every outlet that ran the claim to correct it. Read the statement.
