Press room

For publicists, studios, labels, networks and talent teams. How to get news to Hollywood News Access, how we work, and how to advertise.

Send us news

Tips, exclusives, first looks, red-carpet arrivals, premieres, album drops, casting, and anything the fans should hear first. Every tip is read. Nothing runs until it is confirmed, and we do not name sources who ask not to be named.

Photographs must come with the right to publish them and the photographer's name. We credit every image.

Our newsroom inbox is being set up. Until it is live we are not printing an address here, because an address nobody reads is worse than none.

Standards & corrections

We report what we can confirm. A rumor is not printed as fact. When we carry another outlet's report, we say who reported it and link to them.

Photos are licensed, supplied with permission, or our own. Every image names where it came from. On the wire, an outlet's photo stays credited to that outlet.

The wire is labeled. Wire cards carry another outlet's headline and photo and link out to their story. We never republish their article text.

Paid content says so. Anything a client paid for carries a visible Sponsored label.

Embargoes are hard stops. Nothing publishes before the agreed time.

Corrections are made in the open. When we get something wrong, the story is corrected and dated, and the correction is noted on the page.

Advertise

Display, sponsored features, premiere and red-carpet partnerships, and event coverage. Sponsored content is always labeled. Rate card on request once the advertising desk opens.
